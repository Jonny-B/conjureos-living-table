## Summary

<!-- ADDED: the village name, the names Tobin Hale, Marta Pell and the Copper Kettle, and the five silver pieces are mine. -->

Wick End is a small village where everyone knows everyone. You wake in your home, a young apprentice to your father, the village tinker and smith. Wanting more than his workshop, you take extra jobs on the side. Today the job is the Copper Kettle's cellar: the landlady, Marta Pell, has a rodent problem in her basement and no one to deal with it. Down there you find that the rats are coming in through a tunnel dug by a goblin, who is leading them. This is your first mission. You begin with no armor and no pack, only a simple weapon.

## Truths

<!-- Facts the DM may never contradict. The first four follow the outline. -->

- The story begins in Wick End, a small village. The hero lives there with their father and works as his apprentice.
- The hero takes extra jobs on the side because they want more than their father's workshop.
- The tavern's basement has a rodent problem, and the hero is tasked with cleaning it up. This is the hero's first mission.
- The rats get into the cellar through a tunnel that a goblin dug, and the goblin is the one leading the rats.
- The hero begins with no armor, no potions and no pack. They carry only one simple weapon for their class.

<!-- ADDED: every truth from here down is mine. Please read each as a rule you are happy to have the DM treat as fact. -->

- The hero's father is Tobin Hale, the village tinker and smith. The hero's home is the cottage at the end of the lane, with the workshop beside the bedroom.
- The tavern is the Copper Kettle, the cottage with the barrel and stools outside, across the square from the hero's home. Its landlady is Marta Pell. She pays five silver pieces when the job is done.
- The cellar holds three Rats and two Giant Rats, and the tunnel holds two more Rats and the goblin. These are the only creatures in the adventure.
- The rats are ordinary animals. A Rat is small and weak. A Giant Rat is about the size of a small dog and bites hard. None of them is magical, diseased or able to speak.
- The tunnel mouth is in the east wall of the cellar, behind a heap of sacks. It has been there since the goblin dug it, and it never moves.
- There is exactly one goblin, he is alone apart from his rats, and he dug the tunnel from a den under the old fallow field behind the Copper Kettle.
- The goblin is hungry and clever. He uses the rats to steal the Kettle's flour, cheese and ale from the cellar, and he eats well from it.
- Nobody in Wick End has seen the goblin yet. Nobody knows he exists until the hero finds him.

## DM must

- Keep the tone gentle and small-town. This is a first adventure: a little funny, a little scary, never cruel and never grim.
- Describe the rats honestly: a Rat is a small, weak animal that dies to one hit, and a Giant Rat is a dog-sized animal that bites hard. Never make a rat sound deadlier or harmless than it is.
- Let the hero find the clues for themselves: the scratching under the floor, the fresh earth in the cellar, the scrap of cloth on the sacks.
- Remind the hero, once, that they have no armor and that they can retreat up the stairs to the tavern whenever the cellar goes quiet.
- Let a hurt hero rest in the tavern once the room is quiet, before going back down.
- Voice Marta as warm, brisk and rattled, Tobin as dry and proud, Dunstan as slow and rambling, and the goblin as cocky and cowardly.

## DM never

- Never give the hero armor or a potion before the job is done. The only reward is Marta's five silver pieces, and she pays it afterwards.
- Never move the tunnel, add a second tunnel or add a second way into the cellar.
- Never add any creature that this file does not list: no second goblin, no rat king, no ghosts.
- Never reveal the goblin before the hero finds the tunnel. Rumours of scratching and knocking are fine.
- Never let the hero reach the tunnel before the cellar's rats are dead and the hole is found. The story does not skip ahead.
- Never make the goblin the hero's friend, ally or employer. He may taunt, boast and bargain, but no talk ends the fight in his favour.
- Never kill Tobin, Marta or Dunstan, and never put a village person in danger. This is a first adventure.
