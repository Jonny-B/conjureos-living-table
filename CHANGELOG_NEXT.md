# 0.9.0

The Living Table is now its own game, and it is a whole new way to play.

- Pick a ready-made adventure (The Rat Cellar is first) or a test room, choose a hero, and play it turn by turn on the board.
- Ask the Dungeon Master anything. It answers in the story, and the dice roll on the table.
- Story text now types out one letter at a time. Click once to show the whole page, click again to carry on. You can change the speed in Settings.
- Your adventures are saved to your account, so you can carry on from another device.
- The animated heroes and monsters load in after the table opens.
- Full screen is on the bar at the top.
