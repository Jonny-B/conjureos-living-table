# 0.12.0

The heroes are 3D now, everywhere you see them.

- The hero choice, the character maker, your character sheet and your inventory show the animated 3D figures, not the old pixel drawings.
- The game gets its art ready before the main menu. If it cannot, it says why and offers Retry. On an older ConjureOS phone app it asks you to update the app.
- Anything that has a 3D version is only ever drawn in 3D. The rats, the goblin and some furniture and floors keep their pixel art until they are converted.
- On a tablet the side panel has a little more room, so nothing gets cut off.
